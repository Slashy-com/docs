Claude Desktop and Claude on the web (claude.ai) share the same Connectors panel - the steps below work in both. No config files, no CLI.

<Note>Custom connectors on claude.ai are available on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans.</Note>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Connectors">
    - **Claude Desktop:** click your profile, then **Settings → Connectors**.
    - **Claude on the web:** go to [claude.ai/settings/connectors](https://claude.ai/settings/connectors), or click your profile in the sidebar, then **Settings → Connectors**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mcp/claude-desktop-settings.png" alt="Claude Connectors settings" style={{width: "100%", maxWidth: "800px", margin: "0 auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the custom connector">
    Click **Add Custom Connector** (or **Add custom connector** on the web) and fill in the form:

    - **Name:** `Slashy`
    - **URL:** `https://slashy.ctrlcenter.ai/mcp`

    Click **Add**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mcp/claude-desktop-form.png" alt="Add Custom Connector form" style={{width: "100%", maxWidth: "600px", margin: "0 auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve OAuth">
    Claude opens the Slashy login page. Sign in with Google and click **Allow**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mcp/claude-desktop-oauth.png" alt="Slashy OAuth approval" style={{width: "100%", maxWidth: "800px", margin: "0 auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    Back in Connectors, you should see **Slashy** with a green **Connected** badge. Start a new chat and you'll see Slashy tools available in the tool tray.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/mcp/claude-desktop-verify.png" alt="Slashy connector showing connected status" style={{width: "100%", maxWidth: "800px", margin: "0 auto"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after setup

Claude manages the OAuth token silently and refreshes it automatically. If you sign out of Slashy or revoke access, Claude will prompt you to reconnect.
